Privacy policy

Personal-Dashboard is used by one person, on their own laptop, with their own Google account.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar events: read to show the schedule, and created or changed in one calendar of the user's own for training and study sessions.
  • The app's own hidden Google Drive folder (drive.appdata): to store a backup of the app's settings and data from the user's phone.

What happens to it

The data is processed on the user's own computer. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not sent to any server run by the developer. Access tokens are stored only on that computer.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the developer's GitHub profile.
